Where does the axis start?

Lie factor (Tufte) = the change the reader sees ÷ the change in the data, each as a relative change: (seen ratio − 1) ÷ (data ratio − 1). An honest bar chart has 1.0.

Choosing the window

Try August to October, then October to February. Same station, same year. Data: De Vito, UCI Air Quality, doi:10.24432/C59K5F, CC BY 4.0.

What to change

Line 3: the axis The bars start just below the smaller mean, so the reader compares the leftover tips. For Demo A the second bar looks 3.3 times as tall; the data say 1.3. Use ax.set_ylim(0, None), or points instead of bars.

Line 4: the title “Far worse” is a judgement the figure was drawn to support. State the numbers: “Mean benzene: April 10.4, October 13.5 µg/m³ (+30 %); the middle half of the hours overlaps.”

The code did what it was asked. The request, “show the difference clearly”, was the problem.

Rules for an honest figure

Bars from zero A bar shows a value by its length. Points and lines may use a narrower axis; label it.

Show the spread A mean of 700 hours needs its range: the middle half, or the full distribution.

Say the window State the period and why it was chosen. Show the year if you claim a trend.

Title = finding The title states what the data show, with numbers, not what you hope the reader concludes.

Ask the AI for the figure that answers a question, not for one that “shows the difference clearly”.
