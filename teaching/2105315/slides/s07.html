Activity 1 · Which view answers which question?

Pairs · 10 minutes · no computer needed

When should the morning shift expect the highest benzene?

Hour of day, weekdays only: about 08:00.

Is a Sunday cleaner than a weekday?

Day of week: 6.0 µg/m³ on Sunday against 10 to 12 on weekdays.

Did benzene drop in August?

Year, by month: 7.0 in August against 10 to 13.5 in the months around it.

Was there an unusual event on Thursday last week?

One week, hourly. Monthly means cannot show one day.

Is October worse than April?

Both months, as distributions, not two bars. See the next slide.

Is the station getting worse over the year?

Daily means over the whole year, and say which months you fit. Session 8.

For each question, which of the four views on the previous slide answers it? If none does, say what you would draw instead.

What a mean hides

Two means October 13.5, April 10.4 µg/m³: October is 30 % higher.

The hours 47 % of October’s hours were below April’s mean. In both months the mean is above the median (13.5 against 11.1; 10.4 against 8.5): a few rush hours pull it up.
