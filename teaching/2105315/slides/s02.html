Is missing data random?

One hour of the day loses CO far more often than the rest. A mean of the valid hours under-weights 04:00. Treating −200 as missing is necessary; it does not make the remaining hours a fair sample.

Share of hours with CO(GT) = −200, all 9,357 hours, March 2004 to April 2005. The source does not say why. Data: De Vito, UCI, doi:10.24432/C59K5F, CC BY 4.0.

When is a daily mean worth reporting?

The file covers 15 calendar days. CO was measured on about ten. The 18-hour rule is an assumption for this lab, not a regulation; you will choose your own in Step 8b.

Numbers in each square: valid CO hours that day. Filled: reported. Open: below the minimum. Dashed: no valid hour.
