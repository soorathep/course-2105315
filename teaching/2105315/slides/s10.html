What to change

The sentence “Explains” and “cause” claim a direction the code never tested. For Demo A, r = −0.57 falls to 0.14 once both seasonal trends are removed: r² drops from 32 % to 2 %. The season moved both.

A sentence the data allow “From 20 June to 9 October 2004, cooler days had more NOx (r = −0.57, 77 days). After removing each series’ 29-day trend, r = 0.14. Most of the association follows the season.”

Before any sentence: plot both series over time, then remove what they share.

Mean of the calibration days 0.02, σ = 1.75 µg/m³.

Allowance k = 0.5σ = 0.87. Limit h = 5σ = 8.74.

Sum = max(0, previous sum + difference − mean − k).

The sum passes h on day 23. The last day it was zero, day 19, marks the estimated start: day 20.

A second sum does the same for differences below the mean. Values are rounded to 2 decimals.

Two rules, one drift

3σ rule Alarms on one day far from the mean. Good for a sudden step. For Demo A: day 41, three weeks after the drift began.

CUSUM Adds small differences that keep the same sign. Good for a slow drift. For Demo A: alarm day 23, estimated start day 20.

The cost A smaller k or h alarms sooner, and also alarms more often on noise. The Extension view, Step 9, lets you try.

Both rules need a calibration period that was in control. If the sensor had already drifted then, neither rule can see it.
