2105315 Data Analytics in Chemical Engineering · Weeks 1 to 7

Data Analytics Labs

Each lab asks one question about real plant or monitoring data. You decide what to do with the data, and an AI assistant may write the code. Each lab shows you how to check that code. You submit one file per lab on myCourseVille.

Session slides with narration and captions in English and Thai: Session 1 · Data you can trust, Session 2 · Audit and the AI's code, Session 3 · Why values go missing, Session 4 · Filling gaps you can defend, Session 5 · Spike or real event?, Session 6 · Smoothing without lying, Session 7 · Trends and distributions, Session 8 · Figures that tell the truth, Session 9 · Correlated, or driven?, Session 10 · When did it start?, Session 11 · Did the change work?, Session 12 · Tests on plant data.

  1. Week 1Lab 01 · Is this file ready for the morning report?
    Import settings, missing-value codes, repeated rows    Open
  2. Week 2Lab 02 · Which gap-filling method biases the answer least?
    Kinds of gap, five fill methods, error against the true record    Open
  3. Week 3Lab 03 · Spike or real process event?
    Three outlier rules against known faults, a second sensor, smoothing    Open
  4. Week 4Lab 04 · Does this figure tell the truth about the process?
    Axis baselines and the lie factor, distributions, choosing a trend window    Open
  5. Week 5Lab 05 · Correlated, or driven by a third variable?
    A correlation that is mostly season; when a sensor started to drift (CUSUM)    Open
  6. Week 6Lab 06 · Did the change really shift the process?
    A t-test on hourly values, on daily means, and at every ordinary date in the year    Open
  7. Week 7Lab 07 · From raw file to a defended decision
    Includes joining fast sensor data with a slow lab assay    In preparation
