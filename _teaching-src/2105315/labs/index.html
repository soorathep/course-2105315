Data Analytics in Chemical Engineering

Read plant and monitoring data you can trust, then decide what it shows. An AI assistant may write your code. You learn to check it.

  • 7 weeks
  • 14 sessions of 90 min
  • 7 web labs
  • 1 file per lab on myCourseVille

Start with Session 1Open Lab 01

Two guides: Run AI-written code in Google Colab (before your first homework) · Statistics refresher (before Week 5)

How each week works

  1. 1

    First session

    Concepts and an activity in class, then the first steps of the lab on your own data set.

  2. 2

    Homework

    Download your file, ask an AI assistant for code, read it, and run it in Google Colab. Note three numbers.

  3. 3

    Second session

    Check the AI's code, enter your numbers, finish the lab, and upload one study file.

Slides play with English narration and English or Thai captions; use the buttons at the bottom left of each slide. Labs work on a laptop or a phone. Your student ID picks your own data set.
